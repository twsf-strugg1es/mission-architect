Read it in one go, fast but clear. Aim for about 25 seconds; it must stay under 28 seconds.

How to record
- One file for this video. A quiet room. Phone about 20 cm from your mouth.
- Read the whole text in one go, word for word.
- If you make a mistake, pause 2 seconds, then read the whole text again.
- Say OSDR as "O-S-D-R", DONKI as "DON-kee", JPL as "J-P-L", Bennu as "BEN-oo", delta-v as "delta vee".
